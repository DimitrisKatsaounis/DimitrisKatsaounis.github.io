Reality

Trust

Finance & Accounting Reconciliation Workflow

Automated Invoice & Statement Reconciliation

Pain Point: Staff spend hours manually matching invoices to bank statements, creating “month‑end crunch” and fatigue.

AI Routine: AI scans invoices, extracts data, maps transactions to the correct accounts, and only flags discrepancies for human review.

Gains: Around 80–85% less manual reconciliation effort. Finance teams move from data entry to analysis and strategy.

Case Insight External Study

Operations & Logistics Support Ticket Triage

AI‑Assisted Ticket Sorting & Responses

Pain Point: Teams are buried in repetitive status requests and policy questions, delaying complex issue resolution.

AI Routine: An AI assistant categorizes incoming tickets, drafts first responses from existing knowledge, and routes urgent items to humans.

Gains: Around 30% productivity boost on routine tickets with high accuracy, while staff keep full control of complex cases.

Case Insight External Study
AI drafts and sorts; humans handle nuance, escalation, and relationships.
Professional Services Legal / Non‑Profit

Document Summaries & Research Drafts

Pain Point: Reviewing and drafting long documents eats into time that could be spent with clients or communities.

AI Routine: AI summarizes regulations or past files and produces structured drafts that professionals refine and finalize.

Gains: Work completed about 25% faster, with higher perceived quality and far less “blank page” stress.
